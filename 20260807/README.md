
# SINICA NCHC CUDA-Q Agentic Coding Bootcamp

## Overview / 活動說明

Together with NVIDIA and the OpenACC organization, the Academia Sinica, Research Center for Critical Issues and National Center for High-performance Computing, (NCHC) will host the in-person SINICA NCHC CUDA-Q Agentic Coding Bootcamp on August 7.

SINICA NCHC CUDA-Q Agentic Coding Bootcamp is a hands-on training program designed to help developers explore the latest NVIDIA quantum computing platform and hybrid quantum-classical workflows from quantum programming fundamentals to advanced application development with agentic AI. Through expert-led sessions and practical labs, participants will explore quantum-GPU supercomputing through practical work with CUDA-Q and NVIDIA Ising, experience a live demonstration of agentic AI for autonomous calibration on real quantum hardware from AS, and co-program NVIDIA&reg; CUDA-Q&trade; simulations on advanced GPUs powered by NCHC services.

This bootcamp provides an end-to-end introduction to the NVIDIA quantum computing ecosystem—from development environment setup and fundamental programming concepts to advanced application development and real-world quantum optimization workflows. Participants will also learn how to integrate CUDA-Q with NCHC RAP services and explore practical use cases with superconducting quantum processors.

By attending this bootcamp, participants will:

 -  Learn the fundamentals of the NVIDIA Quantum Platform, including CUDA-Q, NVIDIA Ising, and the vision of quantum-GPU supercomputing;
 -  Gain hands-on experience with CUDA-Q through guided labs, environment setup, and interactive calibration playgrounds powered by NVIDIA NIM;
 -  Explore real-world quantum computing workflows, including CUDA-Q application development, QPU-GPU integration, and agentic calibration for real quantum hardware;
 -  Learn how to leverage agentic AI powered by NCHC RAP services to accelerate quantum programming with CUDA-Q;
 -  Discover advanced CUDA-Q programming techniques, including Bring-Your-Own-Code session and emerging agentic coding capabilities for future quantum applications.

## Materials / 教材

### Hands-on Lab / 實作教材

 -  [CUDA-Q Agentic Coding Bootcamp hands-on repository](https://github.com/Squirtle007/cudaq-agentic-coding/tree/opencode-nchc-rap)

The hands-on lab uses coding agents such as OpenCode, Claude, or Codex to write, run, and verify GPU-accelerated CUDA-Q programs. It begins with CUDA-Q fundamentals and NVIDIA Ising Calibration, then advances a QAOA map-coloring workflow from state-vector simulation to tensor-network and matrix product state methods, with Bring-Your-Own-Code and reusable agent-skill exercises.
