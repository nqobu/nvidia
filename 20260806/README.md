# NVIDIA Tech Sharing and Judging for HiPAC 2026 Finals

## Overview / 活動說明

NVIDIA supported the final day of NCHC HiPAC 2026 on August 6 through a technical sharing workshop and judging. The workshop introduced NVIDIA's core values and evolution into an AI infrastructure company, followed by the NVIDIA platform for science and its accelerated computing, AI, agentic AI, and quantum computing capabilities.

## NVIDIA Contributors

-  **Tech Sharing Workshop:** _Jay Chen_ — NVIDIA Core Values and NVIDIA Platform for Science
-  **Judge:** _Dr. CK Lee_

## Event Information / 活動資訊

-  **Finals:** August 4–6, 2026
-  **NVIDIA Participation:** August 6, 2026
