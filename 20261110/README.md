# NCHC &times; CMU Digital Biology Bootcamp 2026 / 數位生物技術訓練營

## Overview / 活動說明

China Medical University (CMU) and the National Center for High-performance Computing (NCHC), together with NVIDIA and OpenACC, will host this in-person bootcamp on November 10, 2026. The workshop introduces AI workflows for drug discovery with NVIDIA BioNeMo, cellular image analysis with Vista2D and MONAI, and single-cell analysis. Participants will also explore how agentic AI can assist with coding during the hands-on sessions.

本次實體訓練營由中國醫藥大學、國家高速網路與計算中心，攜手 NVIDIA 與 OpenACC 舉辦，透過實作認識 BioNeMo 藥物探索、Vista2D 與 MONAI 細胞影像分析，以及單細胞分析，並介紹 Agentic AI 輔助程式開發。
