# HiPAC 2026 Pre-Competition Training - NVIDIA Sharing

## Overview / 活動說明

During the online [HiPAC 2026](https://event1.nchc.org.tw/2026/hipac/) pre-competition training hosted by the National Center for High-performance Computing (NCHC) on **July 10, 2026**, NVIDIA shared practical approaches to accelerated scientific computing across quantum computing, large language model inference, and HPC with AI Physics.

HiPAC is an application performance optimization competition that challenges participants to identify performance bottlenecks and apply parallel computing and acceleration techniques to real-world HPC and AI workloads. This NVIDIA workshop connected the competition tracks with relevant programming models, runtimes, libraries, profiling tools, and Physics AI workflows.

## Event Information / 活動資訊

 -  **Date / 活動日期**: July 10, 2026
 -  **Time / 活動時間**: 10:00 AM -- 4:00 PM (Taiwan time)
 -  **Format / 活動形式**: Online
 -  **Event / 活動網站**: [HiPAC 2026](https://event1.nchc.org.tw/2026/hipac/)
 -  **Training announcement / 培訓公告**: [HiPAC 2026 finalists and pre-competition training information](https://event1.nchc.org.tw/2026/hipac/news_content4.html)

## Topics / 分享主題

| Track			| Speaker	| Coverage |
| -----			| -------	| -------- |
| Quantum		| Pika Wang	| Quantum-GPU supercomputing, the NVIDIA Quantum Platform, CUDA-Q and cuQuantum, GPU-accelerated simulation and scaling, and DGX Spark for quantum computing |
| LLM			| Cliff Chiu	| LLM inference optimization across scheduling, KV cache management, kernel fusion, low precision and speculative decoding; vLLM and TensorRT-LLM runtimes; TTFT, TPOT and throughput benchmarking |
| HPC & AI Physics	| Jay Chen	| Heterogeneous programming, NVIDIA HPC SDK, GPU-accelerated math libraries and Nsight profiling; Quantum ESPRESSO and AI for materials science; PhysicsNeMo, PINNs and Physics AI surrogate workflows |
